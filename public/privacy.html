1. Introduction & Statutory Framework

This Privacy Policy governs the PRERNA: Self-Discovery Sanctuary application (package: com.prerna.sanctuary), developed to provide transparent, culturally-grounded holistic growth tools for adolescents, young adults, parents, and educators in India.

PRERNA is architected to comply strictly with India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (Act No. 22 of 2023), the DPDP Rules 2025, and international child data protection frameworks including COPPA and the Google Play Families Policy.

2. Section 9 Child Protection Guarantees (Under-18 Users)

In accordance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act 2023, PRERNA enforces the following inviolable technical constraints for all accounts declared as under 18:

  • Zero Behavioral Tracking & Profiling (§9(3)): No behavioral monitoring, longitudinal tracking, or profiling code paths exist. The platform contains zero third-party analytics trackers, zero advertising SDKs, and does not request or access Android's Advertising ID (AD_ID).
  • Zero Targeted Advertising (§9(3)): PRERNA is 100% ad-free. No advertising frameworks are integrated into the application.
  • Verifiable Parental Consent (§9(1)): Under-18 accounts gate child-mode initialization until verifiable parental consent is established. Parents retain full visibility into consented scopes.
  • Protection Against Harmful Processing (§9(2)): All self-discovery activities use non-pathologizing, validated psychometric adaptations (IPIP Big Five and O*NET RIASEC) with transparent plain-language disclosures shown before any session begins.

4. Emergency Crisis Protocol & Clinical Escalation

PRERNA provides 24/7 always-on crisis resources (National Emergency 112, Tele-MANAS 14416, Childline 1098, KIRAN 1800-599-0019, Vandrevala Foundation 9999 666 555) accessible across all views.

In accordance with our Child Safety Policy and Global Rule 0.3, guardian notification is never executed autonomously by algorithms. Any crisis alert requires:

  • Review by an authorized, licensed mental health clinician with verified registration credentials.
  • Confirmation that the adolescent has been informed first (teen_informed_at timestamp).
  • Encrypted transmission exclusively to verified, active guardian relationships established through mutual consent.

5. Statutory Data Subject Rights (DPDP Sections 11, 12, 13)

  • Right to Data Portability (§11): Users can export a complete, machine-readable JSON archive of all on-device self-discovery data at any time via Settings → Data Sovereignty Vault.
  • Right to Permanent Erasure (§12): Users can execute permanent, complete deletion of all personal records, sessions, relationships, and encryption keys with a single click. Erasure purges all 13 SQLite tables and terminates all active sessions immediately.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal (§13): Any privacy inquiry, clarification, or deletion request is responded to within 72 hours.
