# Ethics & Safeguarding Gate (Phase 4.0)

## Overview
This document establishes the formal human-subjects safety gate required before recruiting any minors for usability testing of PRERNA. It is aligned with the ICMR National Ethical Guidelines for Biomedical Research Involving Children.

**IMPORTANT: This is NOT a product feature. This is the institutional and ethical research framework.**

## 1. Study Purpose & Scope
The purpose of this usability study is to evaluate the comprehension, privacy expectations, and workflow discoverability of the PRERNA interface among teenagers, guardians, and safety reviewers. The study will NOT evaluate the clinical efficacy of the AI nor its real-world crisis detection accuracy. 

## 2. Participant Recruitment
- **Age Range**: Adolescents (13-17) and their legally authorized guardians.
- **Inclusion Criteria**: Fluent in English/Hindi, capable of providing assent, with a guardian capable of providing permission.
- **Exclusion Criteria**: Individuals currently experiencing acute psychiatric distress or requiring active clinical intervention.

## 3. Consent, Assent, and Withdrawal
**Crucial Distinction: Product Consent vs. Research Consent**
- **Research Consent**: The guardian must explicitly consent to their child participating in a research study evaluating software.
- **Minor Assent**: The teen must explicitly provide their own assent to participate in the research.
- **Product Verification**: Completely separate from the above, testing the product's simulated verification flow is part of the study task itself.
- **Right to Stop**: Participants may withdraw from the study at any time, for any reason, without penalty. Refusing research participation does not impact their ability to access the final product.

## 4. Privacy and Data Minimization Plan
- **Data Collected**: Task completion status, time on task, qualitative interview responses, and comprehension test scores.
- **Data Not Collected**: Actual psychological distress data. The study uses strictly non-sensitive, synthetic topics (e.g., "choosing between school subjects") to prevent accidental collection of PII or protected health information (PHI).
- **Session Recording**: If screen recording is used, it will capture only the UI interactions. No webcams or audio will be recorded unless explicitly consented to under a separate media release.
- **Confidentiality Limits**: If a participant spontaneously discloses intent to harm themselves or others, researchers are legally mandated to report this to the appropriate authorities, breaking research confidentiality.

## 5. Distress and Escalation Protocol
- **No Intentional Crisis Induction**: Under no circumstances will researchers prompt teenagers to describe real-world trauma, self-harm, or severe distress.
- **Adverse Event Procedure**: If a participant exhibits distress during the study:
  1. The session is immediately paused.
  2. The supervising researcher (who must have appropriate training) will assess the situation.
  3. The guardian will be notified immediately.
  4. The participant will be offered referral to standard care resources (e.g., local crisis hotlines, counselors).
- **Session Supervision**: All teen sessions must be supervised by a researcher trained in safeguarding and adolescent interviewing.
