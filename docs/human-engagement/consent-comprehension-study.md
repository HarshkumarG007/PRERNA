# Data-Practice Comprehension Study (Phase 4.2)

## Overview
This study evaluates whether participants genuinely understand PRERNA's data practices, moving beyond legal checkboxes to test actual comprehension of data collection, storage, and rights. It replaces the concept of "DPDP Comprehension" with practical data-practice understanding.

## 1. Study Focus
The study tests comprehension of PRERNA's explanations regarding:
- What data is collected
- Why it is collected
- Where it is stored
- Who can access it
- How consent works
- How withdrawal/deletion works

## 2. Teach-Back Evaluation Protocol
After the onboarding and consent flows, the moderator will use teach-back questions. 

**Q1: Collection & Purpose**
- *Prompt*: "Can you explain in your own words what kind of information PRERNA collects about you and why?"
- *Classification*: Correct / Partially Correct / Incorrect / Unsure

**Q2: Storage Location**
- *Prompt*: "If you uninstall PRERNA and throw away your phone, what happens to the data PRERNA collected?"
- *Classification*: Correct (It is lost because it is stored locally) / Incorrect (It is in the cloud)

**Q3: Consent Mechanisms**
- *Prompt*: "How did you give permission for PRERNA to run, and how would you stop giving that permission?"
- *Classification*: Correct / Partially Correct / Incorrect / Unsure

## 3. Evaluation Criteria
- **Threshold**: No participant should hold a critical misconception about data retention (e.g., believing data is sold to third parties or permanently stored in a corporate cloud).
- **Redesign Trigger**: If participants repeatedly fail to explain *how* to delete their data, the consent UI must be redesigned.
