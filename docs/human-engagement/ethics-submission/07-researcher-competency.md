# 07. Researcher Competency

**Status:** DRAFT — NOT APPROVED

## 1. Requirement for Competency
Interacting with adolescents in a research setting, particularly concerning a digital mental wellbeing and self-discovery application, requires specific competencies to ensure participant safety, data integrity, and ethical conduct.

## 2. Minimum Training Requirements
All researchers and moderators interacting directly with minor participants must complete and provide certification for the following before any study sessions commence:
- **Ethics Training:** Completion of a recognized course on human subject protection (e.g., CITI Program, ICMR Good Clinical Practice (GCP) or equivalent).
- **Protocol-Specific Training:** Documented review and comprehension of this ethics submission package, specifically the Standardized Moderator Script (Protocol 01, Section 8).
- **Safeguarding Training:** Familiarity with local child protection protocols and the study's specific Adverse Event Reporting Pathway and Sensitive-Content Hard Stop (Protocol 06).

## 3. Moderator Role Definition
The moderator's role is strictly defined as a usability facilitator. 
- The moderator **is not** acting in a clinical, therapeutic, or counseling capacity, regardless of their professional background outside of the study.
- Moderators must adhere strictly to the neutral probes provided in the protocol to prevent accidental psychological interviewing.

## 4. Oversight
The Principal Investigator is responsible for maintaining a log of all researchers' training certifications and ensuring compliance throughout the duration of the Formative Usability Study.
