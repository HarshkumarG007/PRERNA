# 08. Researcher Training

All moderators and researchers interacting with minors during this study must meet the following minimum training requirements:

1. **Adolescent Interviewing**: Demonstrated competency in conducting non-leading usability interviews with adolescent populations, ensuring appropriate tone and pacing.
2. **Safeguarding Protocol**: Complete comprehension of the Hard-Stop rule (Document 06) and immediate recall of the escalation pathway.
3. **Ethics Certification**: Completion of institutionally mandated human-subjects research training (e.g., CITI Program, ICMR Bioethics certification).
4. **Data Privacy**: Training on the Data Management Plan (Document 05) to ensure no accidental retention of sensitive data occurs.
5. **Mock Sessions**: Must complete at least 2 mock sessions with the Principal Investigator serving as a proxy participant to validate non-leading question delivery.
