# 06. Safeguarding and Escalation

## The Hard-Stop Rule
This protocol applies to unexpected, spontaneous disclosures of imminent danger or severe psychological distress during a usability session. **Moderators must not improvise a clinical response.**

## Authority Chain & Escalation Pathway

1. **Unexpected Disclosure Occurs**
   - Participant mentions self-harm, harm to others, or abuse.
2. **Moderator Pauses Session**
   - Moderator calmly pauses the task: "I want to pause here for a moment because your safety is the most important thing to us."
3. **Moderator Alerts Safeguarding Lead**
   - The moderator immediately contacts the designated Safeguarding Lead.
4. **Safeguarding Lead Assesses & Determines Escalation**
   - The Safeguarding Lead (a licensed clinician or trained MSW) assesses the disclosure against the institutionally approved escalation pathway.
5. **Guardian Notification & External Escalation**
   - If imminent danger is identified, the guardian is notified, and if necessary, external emergency services or mandated reporting channels are activated.
6. **Documentation**
   - Only the *minimum necessary information* required to facilitate the escalation is documented. The session is permanently terminated.

## Edge Case Handling
- **Safeguarding Lead Unavailable**: The session is terminated immediately and the secondary clinical contact or emergency services are contacted directly if imminent risk is present.
- **Participant Wants to Leave**: The participant is allowed to leave. The guardian is notified.
- **Disclosure Concerns the Guardian**: If the disclosure involves abuse by the guardian, the guardian is NOT notified. The Safeguarding Lead directly contacts child protective services per local mandatory reporting laws.
