# 07. Adverse Event Procedure

An adverse event is any untoward or unexpected incident occurring during the research study.

## Event Categories & Procedures

| Category | Definition | Who Stops Session? | Who is Notified? | Documentation | Resumption Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Minor Discomfort** | Frustration with UX, mild boredom. | Participant or Moderator | Note in file | Standard research notes | Moderator (after checking in) |
| **Significant Distress** | Crying, visible anxiety, verbalizing distress. | Moderator | Safeguarding Lead | Adverse Event Form | Safeguarding Lead (usually terminates) |
| **Privacy Incident** | Accidental exposure of PII to unauthorized person. | Moderator | Principal Investigator | Privacy Incident Report | Principal Investigator |
| **Accidental Sensitive Data** | Teen types real PHI into the synthetic prompt. | Moderator | Data Manager | Note for immediate secure wipe | Moderator (after wiping screen) |
| **Unexpected Safety Disclosure**| Spontaneous mention of self-harm / abuse. | Moderator | Safeguarding Lead & Guardian (if safe) | Safeguarding Incident Report | Terminates permanently |
| **Technical Failure** | App crashes causing data loss or confusion. | Moderator | Engineering Team | UX Findings Log | Moderator (if app recovers) |
| **Protocol Deviation** | Moderator asks leading questions or skips consent. | Observer/Moderator | Principal Investigator | Deviation Report | PI |
