# 10. Consent Materials

**IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:**
> This study is designed to evaluate usability and comprehension. Participation is not medical treatment and should not be understood as providing therapeutic or clinical benefit.

## Draft Guardian Permission Form (Excerpt)
"Your child is invited to participate in a usability study for PRERNA, a software prototype. The purpose of this study is to see if the interface is easy to use and if the privacy settings are understandable. This is not a clinical trial and PRERNA is not providing medical treatment or therapeutic care. Your child's participation is entirely voluntary. If you say yes, but your child says no, they will not participate. They can stop at any time without any penalty."

## Draft Teen Assent Form (Excerpt)
"We are building an app called PRERNA and we want to know if it makes sense to you. You will be asked to create a test account, do a quick activity about school subjects, and tell us what you think about the privacy screens. This isn't therapy or medical care. You don't have to do this if you don't want to. Even if your parents said it was okay, you can still say no. If you start and decide you want to stop, just tell us, and we'll stop immediately. We will take notes on what you say, but we won't share your name."
