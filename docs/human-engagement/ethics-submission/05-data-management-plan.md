## Participant Withdrawal Data Pathway
- **Withdrawal During Session**: The participant may withdraw at any time. Any data collected up to that point will be immediately destroyed in front of the participant if requested, or destroyed immediately following the session.
- **Withdrawal Post-Session**: If a participant requests their data be removed after the study has concluded, the PI will locate the anonymized data using the participant key (if one was retained) and securely wipe all associated records from the research database within 72 hours.
