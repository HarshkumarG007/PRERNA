# 01. Research Protocol

**IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:**
> This study is designed to evaluate usability and comprehension. Participation is not medical treatment and should not be understood as providing therapeutic or clinical benefit.

## Methodology Overview
The study utilizes a "Vertical Slice" methodology in a controlled, moderated environment. Participants will be guided through non-sensitive, synthetic workflows to evaluate their interaction with the software.

## Participant Tasks
1. **Teen Task**: Navigate the onboarding, complete a non-sensitive academic interest activity, interact with the AI regarding the activity, and review the privacy dashboard.
2. **Guardian Task**: Review the guardian dashboard, identify access boundaries (what they can and cannot see), and navigate the consent revocation flow.
3. **Reviewer Task**: Navigate the safety escalation dashboard using pre-populated, simulated cases (low-risk, ambiguous, high-risk).

## Comprehension Evaluation (Teach-Back)
Instead of standard survey questions, moderators will use teach-back prompts to assess comprehension without leading the participant.
- *Example*: "Imagine you send a private message to PRERNA. Who do you think can read that message?"

## Protocol for Synthetic Interaction
Moderators are strictly instructed to direct teens toward synthetic topics (e.g., "choosing school subjects"). If a teen deviates into highly sensitive personal disclosures, the moderator will gently pause the task and redirect, or invoke the safeguarding protocol if the disclosure involves imminent danger.
