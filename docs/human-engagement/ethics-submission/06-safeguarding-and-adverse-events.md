# 06. Safeguarding and Adverse Events

**Status:** DRAFT — NOT APPROVED

## 1. Safeguarding Principles
While PRERNA is a digital application, the usability study involves direct interaction with adolescents. The safeguarding of the child is the paramount priority, superseding all research objectives.

## 2. Hard-Stop Authority Chain
As defined in the Research Protocol (01, Section 8.7), moderators are equipped with a **Sensitive-Content Hard Stop**. 
- If a participant begins disclosing unexpected, real-life distressing information that is not required by the UI task, the moderator will intervene to protect the participant's privacy and redirect the session.
- Moderators are strictly prohibited from improvising psychological probes or clinical interviewing.

## 3. Unexpected Disclosures of Imminent Harm
If a participant discloses an active, imminent threat to their life, safety, or the safety of others (e.g., active suicidal ideation, ongoing abuse):
1. **Immediate Pause:** The research session is immediately paused.
2. **Notification:** The moderator informs the participant that confidentiality must be broken for their safety.
3. **Escalation:** The moderator immediately contacts the study's **Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL)** and the participant's guardian.
4. **Referral:** The participant and guardian are directed to emergency services or appropriate local clinical resources.

## 4. Adverse Event Reporting Pathway
Any adverse event (e.g., severe distress triggered by a usability task, breach of privacy) must be managed as follows:
1. **Immediate Action:** Ensure the safety and well-being of the participant.
2. **Internal Reporting:** The moderator must report the event to the Principal Investigator within 24 hours.
3. **IEC Notification:** The Principal Investigator must report the adverse event to the reviewing Institutional Ethics Committee (IEC) in accordance with their stipulated timelines (typically within 48 hours for serious events).
4. **Study Pause:** The research protocol will be suspended pending review of the adverse event by the IEC and implementation of corrective actions.
