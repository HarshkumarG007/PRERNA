# 09. Session Script

**Moderator Instructions**: Read bold text verbatim. Do NOT improvise assistance. Do NOT ask leading questions.

## 1. Welcome & Assent Verification
**"Hi, thank you for being here. We are building an app called PRERNA and we want to see if it's easy to use. This isn't a test of you; it's a test of the app. If something is confusing, it's the app's fault, not yours. Before we start, I want to make sure you know that you don't have to do this if you don't want to, and you can stop at any time. Is that okay?"**

## 2. Task Introduction (Synthetic Constraint)
**"Today we're going to pretend you are deciding between three different subjects to study at school next year. We're going to use PRERNA to explore that choice. Please do not enter any real personal problems or sensitive information. Let's start by making an account."**

## 3. Neutral Observation
*(Allow participant to navigate. If they struggle for > 2 minutes, ask:)* **"What are you trying to do right now? What did you expect to happen?"**

## 4. Teach-Back (Post-Task)
*(Do NOT ask leading questions like "You know PRERNA isn't a doctor, right?")*

- **"Who do you think can see the information you just typed in?"**
- **"Where do you think your PRERNA conversation is stored?"**
- **"What information do you think your parent or guardian can access from their phone?"**
- **"What do you think PRERNA is capable of helping you with?"**
- **"If PRERNA detects a serious safety concern, what do you expect to happen next?"**

## 5. Debrief
**"Thank you so much. You really helped us figure out how to make this better."** *(Hand debriefing material).*
