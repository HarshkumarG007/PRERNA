# O*NET Interest Profiler Attribution & Validation

## Source Material
The RIASEC career interest constructs in PRERNA are assessed using items adapted from the **O*NET Interest Profiler**, developed by the National Center for O*NET Development, under sponsorship of the U.S. Department of Labor/Employment and Training Administration.

**Specific Scale Used:** O*NET Interest Profiler Short Form (60-item set).

## Domain Assignment Verification
The adaptation of these items into `onet-riasec-adapted.json` maps directly to the published scoring key for the O*NET Interest Profiler:
- **Realistic:** 10 items (e.g., "Build kitchen cabinets")
- **Investigative:** 10 items (e.g., "Develop a new medicine")
- **Artistic:** 10 items (e.g., "Write books or plays")
- **Social:** 10 items (e.g., "Teach an individual an exercise routine")
- **Enterprising:** 10 items (e.g., "Buy and sell stocks and bonds")
- **Conventional:** 10 items (e.g., "Develop a spreadsheet using computer software")

All items are preserved in their domain assignments to maintain the construct validity of the scoring formula.
