## 1. Executive Summary & Review Purpose

Under **Global Rule 0.7**, an automated AI agent or software engineer is strictly prohibited from self-attesting psychometric construct validity or linguistic equivalence. Psychological instruments translated across cultural contexts risk construct drift, differential item functioning (DIF), and linguistic ambiguity.

This dossier provides a comprehensive, item-by-item audit of the adapted Hindi translations developed for PRERNA. It is prepared for an independent academic psychometrician or bilingual linguist (Hindi/English) to evaluate whether the Hindi items preserve:
1. **Semantic Equivalence:** The translated Hindi text evokes the exact psychological construct measured by the original English item.
2. **Cultural Resonance & Age Appropriateness:** Idioms, examples, and sentence structures match the lived reality of Indian adolescents (13–19 years) without introducing socioeconomic or urban/rural bias.
3. **Non-Stigmatizing & Growth-Oriented Framing:** Avoiding deficit-based or pathologizing psychiatric vocabulary.
4. **ITC Guidelines Alignment:** Adherence to the *International Test Commission (ITC) Guidelines for Translating and Adapting Tests (Second Edition, 2017)*.

## 4. 16-Personality Typology: Indian Cultural Localization

The 16 personality types derived from Big Five empirical projection (McCrae & Costa, 1989) are grouped into 4 quadrants. Rather than using literal or bureaucratic translations, PRERNA utilizes evocative, culturally grounded Sanskrit/Hindi nomenclature that inspires adolescent self-worth:

### Quadrant Classifications:
1. **Analysts (NT):** **विश्लेषक** (*Vishleshak*) — Rationality, intellectual depth, systems innovation.
2. **Diplomats (NF):** **राजनयिक** (*Rajnayik*) — Empathy, idealistic purpose, human flourishing.
3. **Sentinels (SJ):** **संरक्षक** (*Sanrakshak*) — Order, duty, steady execution, institutional loyalty.
4. **Explorers (SP):** **अन्वेषक** (*Anveshak*) — Agility, hands-on mastery, spontaneous creative action.

## 5. Sant Kabir Das *Atma-Jnana* Spiritual Philosophy

PRERNA roots its youth self-discovery not in Western competitive narcissism, but in the classical Indian egalitarian philosophy of Sant Kabir Das:

> **सोरठा / दोहा:**  
> *"जैसे मृगा नाभि कस्तूरी, बन-बन फिरत उदासी।  
> जल में मीन प्यासी, मोहे सुन-सुन आवत हाँसि।।  
> विधि हरि हर जाको ध्यान धरत हैं, मुनिजन सहज अठ्ठासी।  
> सोई हंस तेरे घट माहि, अलख पुरुष अविनाशी।।"*

### Core Tenet:
**"आत्म-साक्षात्कार ही ईश्वर-साक्षात्कार है" (Self Realisation is God's Realisation)**  
- The musk deer wanders hopelessly through forests seeking the scent that resides in its own navel.
- Similarly, adolescents wander through external validation and anxiety, unaware that their inherent superpowers, inner stillness, and potential already reside within (*घट माहि*).
- **Clinical/Pedagogical Objective:** Grounds adolescent self-discovery in timeless peace, reducing exam anxiety, FOMO, and comparative despair.
