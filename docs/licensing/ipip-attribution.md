# IPIP Item Bank Attribution & Validation

## Source Material
The Big Five personality constructs in PRERNA are assessed using items adapted from the **International Personality Item Pool (IPIP)**, developed by Lewis R. Goldberg and managed by the Oregon Research Institute.

**Specific Scale Used:** IPIP Big-Five Factor Markers (50-item set).

## Licensing & Reuse
The IPIP is explicitly placed in the **public domain**. The items are free to copy, edit, translate, and use for any purpose without requiring permission or payment of royalties. 

## Academic Citation
In accordance with good academic practice, we attribute the source items to:
> Goldberg, L. R., Johnson, J. A., Eber, H. W., Hogan, R., Ashton, M. C., Cloninger, C. R., & Gough, H. G. (2006). *The international personality item pool and the future of public-domain personality measures.* Journal of Research in Personality, 40(1), 84-96.

## Domain Assignment Verification
The adaptation of these items into `ipip-bigfive-adapted.json` maps directly to the published scoring key for the 50-item IPIP Big-Five Factor Markers:
- **Extraversion:** 10 items (e.g., "Am the life of the party")
- **Agreeableness:** 10 items (e.g., "Sympathize with others' feelings")
- **Conscientiousness:** 10 items (e.g., "Get chores done right away")
- **Neuroticism (Emotional Stability):** 10 items (e.g., "Have frequent mood swings")
- **Openness to Experience (Intellect/Imagination):** 10 items (e.g., "Have a vivid imagination")

All positive and negative (reverse-scored) keyed items are strictly preserved in their domain assignments to maintain the construct validity of the scoring formula.
