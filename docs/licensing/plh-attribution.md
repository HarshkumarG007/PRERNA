# Parenting for Lifelong Health (PLH) Attribution

PRERNA's parent guide insights are informed by the core principles of the WHO/UNICEF Parenting for Lifelong Health (PLH) initiative.

## Licensing Status
PLH materials are open-source and intended for wide distribution to support positive parenting. However, to ensure strict compliance with any NoDerivatives (ND) clauses in specific PLH materials, PRERNA does **not** ingest verbatim PLH manuals into its RAG corpus.

Instead, PRERNA utilizes original, synthesized summaries of general positive-parenting principles (e.g., active listening, validation, collaborative problem-solving) that are broadly established in clinical literature, including PLH. 

Therefore, no direct redistribution of restricted licensed material occurs.
