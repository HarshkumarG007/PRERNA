# Parenting for Lifelong Health (PLH) Licensing Verification

## Verification Objective
Determine whether the WHO/UNICEF Parenting for Lifelong Health (PLH) materials can be redistributed verbatim in the `data/parenting-guidance/` RAG corpus.

## Findings
WHO and UNICEF typically license their materials under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 IGO (or similar non-commercial, share-alike terms). While this permits non-commercial redistribution, verbatim inclusion of these materials binds the repository to the NonCommercial and ShareAlike clauses.

## Resolution
In accordance with the project's explicit RAG corpora policy, **verbatim copying of source text is prohibited**. 

Instead, the `parenting-guidance/` RAG corpus will contain **original summaries** of the PLH concepts, written specifically for PRERNA. These summaries will cite the original PLH materials in the `corpus-manifest.json` as their theoretical basis, ensuring intellectual honesty while keeping the PRERNA repository free from verbatim licensing encumbrances.
