# Communication Basics for Parents of Teens

Effective communication is the foundation of a strong parent-teen relationship, especially as teens navigate the complex transition to adulthood.

## Core Techniques:
1. **Active Listening:** Listen to understand, not to respond. Reflect back what you hear without immediate judgment or correction.
2. **Validation:** Acknowledge their feelings even if you disagree with their perspective or choices. (e.g., "I can see why you'd feel upset about that.")
3. **Open-Ended Questions:** Ask questions that require more than a yes/no answer to encourage deeper dialogue.

## Navigating Conflict:
- **Avoid Lecturing:** Teens often tune out long lectures. Keep conversations concise and focused.
- **Timing Matters:** Don't try to have deep conversations when either of you is angry, tired, or stressed.
- **Collaborative Problem Solving:** Instead of dictating solutions, invite them to brainstorm with you. ("How do you think we should handle this?")
