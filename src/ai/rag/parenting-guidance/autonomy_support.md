# Supporting Autonomy in Adolescence

As teens mature, their need for autonomy (the ability to make their own choices and govern themselves) increases significantly. Supporting this need safely is a core task of parenting during this phase.

## Scaffolding Independence:
- **Gradual Release of Responsibility:** Instead of a sudden removal of rules, gradually give teens more control over their decisions (e.g., managing their own homework schedule before expecting them to manage a part-time job).
- **Natural Consequences:** Allow teens to experience the natural consequences of safe, low-stakes mistakes rather than rescuing them immediately.

## Balancing Autonomy and Safety:
- **Clear Boundaries:** Autonomy thrives best within clear, mutually understood boundaries. The rules should focus on health, safety, and respect.
- **Negotiation:** As teens grow older, rules should shift from being dictated to being negotiated. This builds their reasoning skills.
