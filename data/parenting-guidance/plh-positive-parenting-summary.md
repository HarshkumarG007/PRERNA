# Positive Parenting Core Concepts (PLH Framework)

## Summary Overview
Positive parenting approaches emphasize building a strong, trusting relationship between parent and teenager, moving away from punitive measures toward collaborative problem-solving. This summary encapsulates key principles from the WHO/UNICEF Parenting for Lifelong Health (PLH) framework.

## Key Principles
1. **Spend One-on-One Time:** Dedicating focused, uninterrupted time (even just 10-15 minutes) to listen to the teenager builds trust and emotional connection.
2. **Praise the Positive:** Acknowledging specific positive behaviors reinforces them. Teens respond better to recognition of effort than to criticism of failure.
3. **Set Clear, Collaborative Rules:** Adolescents require structure, but they are more likely to respect boundaries if they are involved in setting them. Clear consequences should be established ahead of time.
4. **Manage Stress:** Parents must manage their own stress and emotional regulation first before attempting to correct or guide their teenager.
